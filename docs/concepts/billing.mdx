---
title: Quotes and billing
description: Read request prices, set a spending limit with max_cost, and understand how Jobs are charged.
---

Yir charges only for results it delivers. Before submitting, you can ask for a quote; when you submit, Yir reserves at most one attempt's worth of funds and releases whatever is not used when the Job ends.

## Get a quote

Send the same body you would submit to a quote endpoint:

| Operation | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| One image request | `POST /v1/images/quotes` |
| One video request | `POST /v1/videos/quotes` |
| Up to 20 image or video requests | `POST /v1/quotes` |

Quotes validate the model, input, and parameters exactly like a submission, but create no Job, reserve no funds, and do not lock prices. They reject `max_cost` and `webhook_url`, which belong to submission only.

```json
{
  "object": "quote",
  "model": "openai/gpt-image-2",
  "currency": "USD",
  "supply": { "available": true, "requires_max_cost": false, "issues": [] },
  "primary": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.04" },
  "max": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.05" },
  "official": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.053" },
  "single_attempt_upper_bound": "0.05",
  "has_verifiable_upper_bound": true,
  "expires_at": 1790000300
}
```

The values above are illustrative. Amounts are USD decimal strings; use decimal arithmetic, not floating point.

### Reading the prices

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` | Price of the channel Yir would try first. |
| `max` | Highest price among the channels Yir could use for this request. It is not a sum of attempts. |
| `official` | The model maker's own reference price, for comparison. It does not mean the official channel will run. |
| `price_difference_percent` | Optional comparison with `official`. Negative means Yir is cheaper. |
| `single_attempt_upper_bound` | The most one attempt can cost, verified across all eligible channels. `null` if any channel cannot be bounded. |
| `supply.available` | `false` with `issues: ["no_matching_supply"]` when no channel can run this request right now. |
| `supply.requires_max_cost` | `true` when an eligible usage-billed channel has no verifiable whole-request upper bound. Submit must then include `max_cost` of at least `0.01`. |

Each price has one of three kinds:

- `fixed`: the complete price for the request.
- `estimate`: covers **generated output only**, for usage-billed models (`estimate.output_tokens` or `estimate.output_megapixels` shows the assumption). Input and other charges are excluded, so do not use it as a total or a spending limit.
- `unavailable`: `amount` is `null`. An unknown price is never zero.

## Set a spending limit

`max_cost` on a generation request is the most the whole Job may charge, for example `"0.25"` for US$0.25 (up to six decimal places).

- **Omitted:** Yir uses the highest verified single-attempt ceiling among eligible channels at admission.
- **Required:** when the quote reports `requires_max_cost: true`.
- **Too low:** a limit below the first channel's price is rejected. Channels whose price exceeds the limit are skipped during fallback; when none are left, the Job fails with `YIR_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`.

Submission checks prices again. A quote you obtained earlier does not guarantee the same price or availability, and a price increase never lets a Job charge more than your authorization.

## How a Job is charged

The reservation rules below apply when `billing_mode` is omitted. Explicit actual billing uses the collection rules in the next section.

1. **Admission** reserves one amount: the highest single-attempt ceiling among eligible channels, capped by `max_cost`. Fallback attempts share this reservation; they are never added together.
2. **Success** charges only the attempt whose result was delivered and releases the rest.
3. **Failure or cancellation** releases the whole reservation. The customer charge is zero.
4. **Stop requested** during an attempt: that attempt may still succeed and be charged. See [cancellation](/docs/concepts/task-lifecycle#cancellation).

For usage-billed channels without a verifiable upper bound, Yir reserves the explicit `max_cost` in full. An output-only estimate does not determine this reservation.

## Pay the actual cost

For supported channels, put `"billing_mode": "actual"` on both your quote and generation requests. This explicitly authorizes the complete cost of the successful delivered result, including charges known only after generation. Do not send `max_cost` with this mode: there is no customer spending cap.

The initial supported combinations are Kling 2.6 or 3.0 Motion Control with `routing.only` selecting KIE and/or APIMart, and FLUX 2 Pro image editing with `routing.only: ["fal"]`. You must specify the channels explicitly. Other models or channels reject this mode; normal input and file requirements still apply.

Yir requires a positive available wallet balance and no outstanding debt before admitting a new Job. It does not reserve an estimated total. After successful delivery, Yir records one final charge based on the task's authoritative bill and the discount frozen for that attempt. If your wallet cannot cover it, the unpaid amount becomes Job debt. A future manual recharge repays the oldest debt first; repayment does not charge the same Job again. Outstanding debt blocks new Jobs, while already admitted Jobs continue under their saved authorization. This does not enable automatic card charging.

Failed, cancelled and non-winning attempts remain Yir's cost. Missing or conflicting bills enter billing reconciliation; an unknown bill is never treated as zero or replaced with an estimate. `billing.billing_mode: "actual"` identifies the saved authorization in the Job response. Retrying with the same idempotency key cannot change the billing mode. Existing Jobs and requests that omit this field keep their original billing contract.

Billing is final when the Job reaches a terminal state. Read the terminal Job's `billing` object:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `billing.total_charged_by_yir` | The final amount charged for this Job. |
| `billing.compute_charges` | Compute line items. A new Job has one `managed` item billed by `yir`, with `status` `settled`, or `waived` with amount `0.000` when the charge is waived. The array can be empty. |
| `billing.gateway_fee` | The gateway fee: `amount` and `status` (`settled` or `waived`). |
| `billing.currency` | Always `USD`. |
| `billing.max_cost` | The authorization limit that applied. It is not the charge. |
| `billing.savings` | Optional comparison with the user price frozen at admission. It can be negative after fallback. |
| `billing.official_comparison` | Optional comparison with the complete official price frozen at admission for this request. |

Costs from failed attempts, and from provider results that arrive after Yir has already closed a Job, are absorbed by Yir.

## Account and key limits

- **Balance.** A Job needs enough available balance for its reservation, otherwise it is rejected with `402 YIR_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`. Top up in Console; Yir never charges your payment method automatically. You can set a low-balance email alert on the Billing page.
- **Monthly spend limit per API key.** Optional, set in [Console → API Keys](/docs/console/api-keys). Admission adds the new Job's reservation to the key's settled charges and in-flight reservations for the current UTC month. If the total would exceed the limit, the request fails with `403 YIR_SPEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and no Job is created. Running Jobs are never interrupted.

## Pricing your own product

If you resell generation to your users, keep your retail prices and refund rules separate from Yir's authorization. You can use a `fixed` `primary` price as a pricing input; an `estimate` covers output only and cannot serve as a complete price. Use `single_attempt_upper_bound` for authorization only when `has_verifiable_upper_bound` is `true`; otherwise choose an explicit `max_cost` that your business can accept. Reconcile `total_charged_by_yir` once per Job.
