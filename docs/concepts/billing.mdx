---
title: Quotes and billing
description: Read request price estimates, cap a Job's total charge with max_cost, and understand how Jobs are charged.
---

Yir passes each channel's actual bill through to you. Every attempt the channel actually billed is charged at its upstream cost after your discount, whether it succeeded, failed, or was followed by a fallback; attempts the channel never billed cost nothing, and Yir absorbs its own delivery failures and outcome timeouts. Yir does not reserve or freeze balance. Before submitting, you can ask for a quote to see an estimate.

## Get a quote

Send the same body you would submit to a quote endpoint:

| Operation | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| One image request | `POST /v1/images/quotes` |
| One video request | `POST /v1/videos/quotes` |
| Up to 20 image or video requests | `POST /v1/quotes` |

Quotes validate the model, input, and parameters exactly like a submission, but create no Job, move no funds, and do not lock prices. They reject `max_cost` and `webhook_url`, which belong to submission only.

```json
{
  "object": "quote",
  "model": "openai/gpt-image-2",
  "currency": "USD",
  "supply": { "available": true, "requires_max_cost": false, "issues": [] },
  "primary": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.04" },
  "max": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.04" },
  "official": { "kind": "fixed", "amount": "0.053" },
  "single_attempt_upper_bound": null,
  "has_verifiable_upper_bound": false,
  "expires_at": 1790000300
}
```

The values above are illustrative. Amounts are USD decimal strings; use decimal arithmetic, not floating point.

### Reading the prices

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `primary` | Estimated price on the channel Yir would try first. It is a reference price, not a charge or a ceiling. |
| `official` | The model maker's own reference price, for comparison. It does not mean the official channel will run. |
| `price_difference_percent` | Optional comparison with `official`. Negative means Yir is cheaper. |
| `supply.available` | `false` with `issues: ["no_matching_supply"]` when no channel can run this request right now. |

`max`, `single_attempt_upper_bound`, `has_verifiable_upper_bound`, and `supply.requires_max_cost` are deprecated and kept only for compatibility with earlier clients: `max` repeats `primary`, and the others are always `null`, `false`, and `false`. Do not base decisions on them.

Each price has one of three kinds:

- `fixed`: the channel's listed price for the complete request.
- `estimate`: covers **generated output only**, for usage-billed models (`estimate.output_tokens` or `estimate.output_megapixels` shows the assumption). Input and other charges are excluded.
- `unavailable`: `amount` is `null`. An unknown price is never zero.

Both `fixed` and `estimate` predict the final charge, which follows the channel's actual bill and can differ.

## Set a spending limit

`max_cost` on a generation request is optional. It caps the whole Job's charge, for example `"0.25"` for US$0.25 (up to six decimal places). No model requires it.

- **Omitted:** every channel-billed attempt is charged its actual cost.
- **Set:** Yir only uses channels, including fallback channels, whose estimate fits within what remains of `max_cost` after earlier attempts' charges. The Job's total charge across all attempts never exceeds `max_cost`; if the actual cost is higher, Yir absorbs the difference.
- **Too low:** if no eligible channel's estimate fits, the submission is rejected with `400 YIR_INVALID_REQUEST`. If no further attempt fits after a failure, the Job fails with `YIR_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`.

Submission checks prices again. A quote you obtained earlier does not guarantee the same price or availability.

## How a Job is charged

1. **Admission** checks your live available balance against the first channel's estimate. Nothing is reserved. Before each later attempt, the balance is checked again against that channel's estimate. While your available balance is below US$5 and another of your Jobs is running, a new Job waits in `queued` and rechecks every 10 seconds; if it still cannot start after 30 minutes, it fails with `YIR_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`.
2. **Each attempt the channel billed** is charged when it ends: the channel's actual upstream cost × your discount, rounded up to US$0.000001. This includes the delivered attempt, an attempt that failed before a fallback, and `YIR_CONTENT_REJECTED` output the provider generated and billed but Yir did not deliver. The Job's total is capped by `max_cost` when set.
3. **Attempts the channel never billed** cost nothing, so most failed and cancelled Jobs are free. Yir also absorbs its own failures: a result Yir could not deliver (`YIR_RESULT_DELIVERY_FAILED`) and an attempt whose outcome the channel never confirmed (`YIR_OUTCOME_TIMEOUT`). A failed attempt is charged only from a confirmed channel bill; if the bill never arrives, Yir absorbs it.
4. **Stop requested** during an attempt: that attempt may still succeed and be charged. See [cancellation](/docs/concepts/task-lifecycle#cancellation).
5. **Exceptions.** If the bill for a delivered result is missing or conflicting, or the discounted actual cost is more than three times that attempt's estimate and at least US$0.02 above it, Yir charges the estimate frozen when the attempt was submitted and absorbs the difference.

If your balance cannot cover a charge at settlement, the balance becomes negative. A negative balance blocks new Jobs until a top-up repays it; Jobs already admitted continue. Yir never charges your payment method automatically.

## Pay the actual cost

For supported channels, put `"billing_mode": "actual"` on both your quote and generation requests. This explicitly authorizes the actual cost of every channel-billed attempt, including charges known only after generation. Do not send `max_cost` with this mode: there is no customer spending cap, and an unpaid amount becomes Job debt.

The initial supported combinations are Kling 2.6 or 3.0 Motion Control with `routing.only` set to `["kie"]`, `["apimart"]`, or `["kie", "apimart"]`, and FLUX 2 Pro image editing with `routing.only: ["fal"]`. You must specify the channels explicitly. APIMart Kling Motion Control and FAL FLUX 2 Pro image editing have no pre-generation estimate, so they run only in this mode; KIE Motion Control has an estimate and also works without it. Other models or channels reject this mode; normal input and file requirements still apply.

Yir requires a positive available wallet balance and no outstanding debt before admitting a new Job. Each channel-billed attempt is charged from its authoritative bill and the discount frozen for that attempt, and the Job settles once when it ends. If your wallet cannot cover it, the unpaid amount becomes Job debt. A future manual recharge repays the oldest debt first; repayment does not charge the same Job again. Outstanding debt blocks new Jobs, while already admitted Jobs continue under their saved authorization.

Attempts the channel never billed remain Yir's cost. Missing or conflicting bills enter billing reconciliation; an unknown bill is never treated as zero or replaced with an estimate. `billing.billing_mode: "actual"` identifies the saved authorization in the Job response. Retrying with the same idempotency key cannot change the billing mode.

## Read the final charge

Billing is final when the Job reaches a terminal state. Read the terminal Job's `billing` object:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `billing.total_charged_by_yir` | The final amount charged for this Job. |
| `billing.compute_charges` | Compute line items. A new Job has one `managed` item billed by `yir` that sums every charged attempt, with `status` `settled`, or `waived` with amount `0.000` when nothing was charged. The array can be empty. |
| `billing.gateway_fee` | The gateway fee: `amount` and `status` (`settled` or `waived`). |
| `billing.currency` | Always `USD`. |
| `billing.max_cost` | The cap that applied, if you set one. It is not the charge. |
| `billing.official_comparison` | Optional comparison with the complete official price frozen at admission for this request. |

`billing.savings` is no longer returned; use `official_comparison`. Costs from attempts the channel never billed, and from provider results that arrive after Yir has already closed a Job, are absorbed by Yir.

## Account and key limits

- **Balance.** A Job is rejected with `402 YIR_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` when your available balance does not cover the first channel's estimate, or while your balance is negative. Top up in Console. You can set a low-balance email alert on the Billing page.
- **Monthly spend limit per API Key.** Optional, set in [Console → API Keys](/docs/console#api-keys). Admission adds the new Job's estimate to the key's settled charges for the current UTC month. If the total would exceed the limit, the request fails with `403 YIR_SPEND_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` and no Job is created. Jobs still running are not counted, so concurrent Jobs can take a key slightly past its limit. Running Jobs are never interrupted.

## Pricing your own product

If you resell generation to your users, keep your retail prices and refund rules separate from Yir's charges. A quote's `primary` is a useful pricing input, but the final charge follows the actual cost, and an `estimate` covers output only. Set `max_cost` when you need a hard ceiling per Job. Reconcile `total_charged_by_yir` once per Job, including failed Jobs: a failed Job can carry a non-zero charge when the channel billed an attempt.
