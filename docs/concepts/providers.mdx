title: Providers and managed supply
description: How Yir supplies generation through managed provider accounts, and what that means for your credentials.
---

A **provider** (or channel) is an upstream platform that runs a model: the model maker's own API, or a certified third-party supplier. Yir only lists channels and models that it has integrated and verified.

## Managed supply

Yir Cloud runs every Job on provider accounts that Yir manages and pays for. You fund one Yir wallet; Yir handles provider accounts, capacity, and billing, and passes each channel's actual bill through to you at your discount; attempts the channel never billed cost nothing (see [quotes and billing](/docs/concepts/billing)).

A channel being listed for a model does not guarantee it can run your request right now. Execution depends on the parameters it supports, its current availability, and your `max_cost`, if set. A quote tells you whether any channel can currently serve a specific request.

## Your credentials

- Requests use your Yir API Key only.
- Yir does not accept your own provider keys (BYOK), custom base URLs, custom providers, or model mappings.
- Provider credentials stay on Yir's servers and never appear in responses, logs, or Webhooks.

## Choosing channels

Use the `routing` object to prefer lower prices, restrict a request to specific channels, or pick a channel variant. See [routing and fallback](/docs/concepts/routing).

A channel can offer several versions of a model that differ in price and in the parameters they serve, so the same request may not be possible on every version. See [variants can serve different requests](/docs/concepts/routing#variants-can-serve-different-requests).
