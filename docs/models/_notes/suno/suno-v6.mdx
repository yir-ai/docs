## Lyrics

Choose one of three combinations:

| Goal | `input.lyrics` | `instrumental` | `lyrics_optimizer` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Song with your own lyrics | Required | `false` | `false` |
| Song with lyrics written from the prompt | Omit | `false` | `true` |
| Instrumental | Omit | `true` | `false` |

Other combinations are rejected with `400 YIR_INVALID_REQUEST`. The prompt describes the style (up to 1,000 characters); lyrics are up to 5,000 characters. Extend and stems are not available.

## Cover an uploaded song

Send `input.type: "reference"` with exactly one `reference_audio` source song (up to 8 minutes) to get a cover that keeps its melody in the style the prompt describes. Lyrics use the same three combinations; with `lyrics_optimizer` the model writes lyrics from the prompt and the source song. A cover makes two tracks at the same price as basic generation. See [Cover an uploaded song](/docs/guides/music-generation#cover-an-uploaded-song).

## Output

Every request makes two tracks: `n` is fixed at `2`, and a successful Job returns two MP3 files (`media_type: "audio/mpeg"`) in `result.files`, each with its measured `duration_ms`. There is no duration parameter.

## Price

Priced per request (two tracks): US$0.06 on KIE and US$0.05 on APIMart. The final charge is the amount the channel actually billed.
