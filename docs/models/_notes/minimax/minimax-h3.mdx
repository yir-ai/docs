## Reference video and audio quotes

Reference-to-video bills reference videos by their measured length. Quote accepts public HTTPS `url` references without downloading them, so with a URL reference video or audio the quote reports supply as available, `primary.amount` is `null` and `expected_amount` is absent. This applies to every resolution, including 480p and 1080p. Upload the files and pass their ready `file_id` values when you need the exact amount. Submit imports and measures URL references before admission, and `max_cost` applies to the measured price.
