## Lyrics

Choose one of three combinations:

| Goal | `input.lyrics` | `instrumental` | `lyrics_optimizer` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Song with your own lyrics | Required | `false` | `false` |
| Song with lyrics written from the prompt | Omit | `false` | `true` |
| Instrumental | Omit | `true` | `false` |

Any other combination is rejected with `400 YIR_INVALID_REQUEST`: a vocal track needs lyrics unless `lyrics_optimizer` is on, `instrumental` cannot be combined with lyrics or `lyrics_optimizer`, and your own lyrics cannot be combined with `lyrics_optimizer`. Separate lines with `\n`; structure tags such as `[Verse]` and `[Chorus]` are allowed. Prompts are up to 2,000 characters and lyrics up to 3,500.

## Length and output

There is no duration, tempo or key parameter. The model decides the track length, and the prompt does not guarantee one. Each Job returns one MP3 file (`media_type: "audio/mpeg"`) in `result.files`, with its measured length in `duration_ms`.

## Price

Served through the MiniMax official channel and FAL, both at US$0.15 per track. `lyrics_optimizer` runs only on the MiniMax official channel and adds US$0.01 for writing the lyrics, shown in the quote. FAL needs a prompt of at least 10 characters; Yir routes each request to a channel that accepts it. The final charge is the amount the channel actually billed.
