## Prompt length by channel

KIE and fal accept up to 4,096 Unicode characters after trimming; APIMart accepts up to 8,000. In `reference` mode, the available channels accept at most 8,000 characters, and prompts from 4,097 to 8,000 characters can use APIMart but not fal. Quote the exact request to check eligible channels. Yir does not truncate prompts.
