Send one portrait as `reference_image` and one driving audio as `reference_audio` (wav or mp3, 4–300 seconds, under 15 MB; the portrait must be under 30 MB). The output is 720p, its length follows the audio and its aspect ratio follows the portrait, so `duration` does not change the result.

Pricing is per output second, using the measured audio length rounded up to whole seconds; upload the audio and pass its `file_id` to get a quoted amount. The final charge follows the channel's billing.
