Motion Control is priced by the measured length of the reference video, rounded down to whole seconds; upload it and pass its `file_id` to get a quoted amount.

Complex or fast motion and shot changes can make Kling Motion Control output shorter than the reference video, and consumed charges are not guaranteed to be refunded, subject to channel billing rules.

On fal image-to-video routes, including first-and-last-frame input, the output aspect ratio follows the first frame and the requested `aspect_ratio` is ignored. Quote `parameter_notices` describe expected handling; Job notices describe the channel actually used. Text-to-video still uses the requested aspect ratio.
