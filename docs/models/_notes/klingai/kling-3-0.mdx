Motion Control is priced by the measured length of the reference video, rounded to whole seconds by channel rules (down on APIMart, up on KIE, to the nearest second on fal); upload it and pass its `file_id` to get a quoted amount.

Complex or fast motion and shot changes can make Kling Motion Control output shorter than the reference video, and consumed charges are not guaranteed to be refunded, subject to channel billing rules.

On fal and Higgsfield image-to-video routes, including first-and-last-frame input, the output aspect ratio follows the first frame and the requested `aspect_ratio` is ignored. Quote `parameter_notices` describe expected handling; Job notices describe the channel actually used. Text-to-video still uses the requested aspect ratio.

## Prompt length

Text-to-video and first-frame or first/last-frame image-to-video accept up to 2,500 Unicode characters after trimming. Longer prompts are rejected before submission; Yir does not truncate them. Subject-reference and motion-control requests follow their separate contracts.
