In `image` mode, add up to 8 `keyframe` references between `first_frame` and the optional `last_frame`. Keyframes play in the order they appear in `references` (they carry no timestamp), and the same image may appear more than once to return to a shot. Name frames in the prompt as `@first_frame`, `@keyframe_1`, `@keyframe_2` and `@last_frame`; every name must match a frame in the request.

The total number of frames (first, keyframes and last) is capped by duration: 3 to 8 seconds allow duration + 1 frames (for example 4 frames at 3 seconds), and 9 seconds or longer allow 10. Keyframes cost nothing extra; the price is per output second.
