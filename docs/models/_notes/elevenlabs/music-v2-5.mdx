## Lyrics

ElevenLabs music takes no `input.lyrics`. Choose one of two combinations:

| Goal | `instrumental` | `lyrics_optimizer` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Song with lyrics written from the prompt | `false` | `true` |
| Instrumental | `true` | `false` |

Sending `input.lyrics`, or neither switch, is rejected with `400 YIR_INVALID_REQUEST`. Describe the style, mood and any lyric ideas in the prompt (up to 4,100 characters).

## Length and output

`duration` sets the track length in seconds: `30`, `60`, `120`, `180`, `240` or `300` (default `60`). Each Job returns one MP3 file (`media_type: "audio/mpeg"`) in `result.files`, with its measured length in `duration_ms`.

## Price

Served through FAL at US$0.60 per output minute, rounded up to whole minutes: 30 and 60 seconds cost US$0.60, 300 seconds US$3.00. The quote shows the price for the requested `duration`; the final charge is the amount the channel actually billed. ElevenLabs' official API lists US$0.15 per minute.
