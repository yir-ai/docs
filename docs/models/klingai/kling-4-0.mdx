### Subject references (`reference`)

Give references the same `references[].subject` name to group them into one subject, and refer to it in the prompt as `@<subject>`.

- Photo subjects: up to 7, each with 2 to 4 `reference_image`
- Video subjects: up to 3, each with 1 `reference_video` and no photos
- Voice: a subject may add 1 `reference_audio` as its voice; up to 3 subjects with only `reference_audio` are standalone voices
- Subjects can be combined with ungrouped references; the reference count covers ungrouped references plus subjects, and role limits apply to ungrouped references only.
