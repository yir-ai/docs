[Console → Logs](https://yir.ai/console/logs) lists your Jobs. Use the list to scan outcomes and open a Job for details.

## Job details

When available, the detail page shows:

- the result and its status;
- the channel that produced the result, and whether fallback recovered the Job (counted only when an earlier attempt was confirmed failed and a later one succeeded);
- the total Yir charge, and a comparison with the official reference price when a like-for-like price exists (negative differences are shown as they are; unknown costs are never shown as savings);
- input transfer facts and one execution timeline.

## Privacy

By default, details show the prompt length and the number of references. During the first 7 days you can expand your stored prompt. Reference URLs, file IDs, raw provider responses, credentials, and internal routing data are never shown.

## Retention

Prompts, inputs, and results are kept for 7 days after the Job is created; summary logs for 90 days. Billing records are kept separately. Logs are not an asset library, so download any results you need. See [results and retention](/docs/concepts/task-lifecycle#results-and-retention).
