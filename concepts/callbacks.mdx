When YIR manages a task, YIR is responsible for delivering the source-compatible callback.

Callback URLs must use supported public URL schemes and must not resolve to loopback, private, link-local, localhost, or single-label internal hosts. YIR rejects unsafe callback targets instead of retrying them forever.

Callbacks are retried with a stable event id. Your endpoint should be idempotent.
