---

YIR is a source-compatible generation gateway for image, video, audio, and other long-running multimodal AI tasks.

Instead of replacing your existing provider SDK, YIR lets you keep the upstream request shape and change the base URL. YIR then adds observability, failure protection, cost-aware routing, and delayed hedging when you explicitly enable those modes.
